### Slopes, maxima, and minima 

* Where was the car stopped (zero veolocity)? The points where the tangent is horizontal (derivative is zero). 

* The point where the car is the farthest is where it isn't moving. 
    - If you want to find the max/min of a function, look for the derivative = zero (tangent line horizontal).
